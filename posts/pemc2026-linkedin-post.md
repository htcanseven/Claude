# PEMC 2026 post

Last week I was in Tallinn for IEEE PEMC 2026, the 22nd International Power Electronics and Motion Control Conference.

It was a busy few days. I presented three papers and chaired two sessions, so I got to see the "3 min" card from both sides.

One of the papers was on a PMSM with direct oil-cooled hollow hairpin windings. This work was supported in part by the Academy of Finland's Center of Excellence in High-Speed Energy Conversion Systems and by Danfoss Editron. Many thanks to my co-authors on all three papers.

Thanks also to everyone who came to the talks and asked questions, to the speakers in the sessions I chaired, and to TalTech and the organizing team for a very well-run conference.

And it was great to spend time with colleagues and friends between the sessions and at the gala dinner.
