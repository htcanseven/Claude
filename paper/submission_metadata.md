## Novelty & Significance (under 100 words)

Diagnostic performance is treated as a measured quantity rather than a score.
Random-split evaluation of overlapping windows is shown to overstate
smartphone-MEMS fault-diagnosis accuracy by 41–47 percentage points, so the
near-perfect results routinely reported in this literature are largely a
measurement artefact. Under a leakage-free protocol, performance is reported
with Type A expanded uncertainty and paired significance testing at a defensible
experimental unit. A resource-frugal configuration (25 Hz, three raw channels,
4 s) is measured to outperform full-rate sensing by 23.1 points while cutting
data volume eight-fold, yielding deployment-honest operating points for
low-power IoT condition monitoring.
