# Highlights

*Deployment-Realistic Performance Measurement of Low-Cost MEMS Sensing for
IoT-Based Condition Monitoring of Induction Machines*

Five concise findings (each within the customary 85-character limit):

- Leakage-free evaluation cuts smartphone-MEMS fault accuracy from ~100% to ~53%.
- Random-split optimism gap measured at 41-47 accuracy points at 100 Hz.
- Frugal 25 Hz / 3 raw channels / 4 s node lifts accuracy 23 points (p=0.0001).
- Gravity compensation reshapes, not discards, diagnostic low-frequency content.
- Accuracy-resource trade-off mapped to BLE, Zigbee, NB-IoT and LoRaWAN budgets.
