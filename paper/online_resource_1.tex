\abstract{This resource holds the tables behind statements of the paper that its main text summarises: the distances of every rule and per characteristic, the simulation dataset against the measurement and the redundancy of the scans, the fitted Gaussian processes with a sensitivity analysis of the trend GP's priors, the predictors crossed with relation-matched intervals and the coverage of every interval rule, the guard bands available when deciding, the costs, the between-run sensitivity with the trial, and the cross-fitted selection. \maketitle

\section{How to read the tables}

The relations and rules are those of the paper (its Table~2 and Section~3.3). A new design is judged with a produced \emph{sibling} (the alternative alone held out), at a new process setting that is \emph{interpolated} (300\,kN held out) or \emph{extrapolated} (100 or 500\,kN held out), or as a \emph{new family} (one geometry calibrates the rules for the other). Distances are in within-run floors of the characteristic's own geometry, a new family in the floor of the produced family unless stated otherwise; ``$<$1'' marks distances below one floor, and a pair $a/b$ is the decisive and the safe distance. P+RM is the point prediction of rule P with the relation-matched interval; M3n is named M4 in the code. The reference prior of requirement distances weights the 47 distances of the evaluation grid within $\pm20$ floors equally (0, $\pm0.5$ to $\pm10$ in steps of 0.5, $\pm12$, $\pm15$ and $\pm20$), the release-like prior the grid within $\pm5$ floors.

\section{Distances}

Table~\ref{tab:s-all} gives every rule in every relation, with the interval of its decisive distance and a new family also in its own floor, and Table~\ref{tab:s-qc} both distances per characteristic for the main rules and M7. On the cup depth, M1s and M7 falsely accept extrapolated alternatives up to 165 and 118 floors from their limit.

\section{Simulation and measurement}

Table~\ref{tab:s-validation} compares the simulation dataset with the measurement in every cell, at the nominal friction and at the closest grid friction, with the extraction noise of the simulated characteristics; Table~\ref{tab:s-scan} gives the redundancy of the scans per series and the components of the floor.

\section{Learning and intervals}

Table~\ref{tab:s-gp} gives the fitted Gaussian processes by relation and Table~\ref{tab:s-priors} the trend GP M7n with each prior median halved or doubled. Table~\ref{tab:s-crossing} crosses eight point predictors with the relation-matched interval and sets them against the rules' own intervals and against M7n calibrated on both families. Tables~\ref{tab:s-coverage} and \ref{tab:s-coverage-detail} give the coverage of every interval rule, overall and per family, characteristic and decision-time stratum.

\section{Guard bands}

Table~\ref{tab:s-guards} gives the guard bands a team can have when it decides, under both priors, with intervals, verdict counts, the worst characteristic and the share correct when a force cell of the family's own production is left out instead; Table~\ref{tab:s-guard-qc} gives the share correct per characteristic, and Table~\ref{tab:s-prior} the band and trial rate against the width of the prior.

\section{Costs, between-run variation and selection}

Table~\ref{tab:s-costs} gives the cheapest option for every relation, trial cost, prior and ratio of false-reject to false-accept cost; Table~\ref{tab:s-between} the distances and bands with a between-run component added to the truths, and the trial under the same component; Table~\ref{tab:s-crossfit} the rules and bands selected on one geometry and evaluated on the other.

\section{Development record}

M0--M5 and M3n were defined before the first results; everything else was added afterwards, most of it in response to reviews of earlier versions simulated with a large language model, which are not reviews by a journal. Four simulated rounds led to M0w, Mc, M1n, NN, M2n, M5n, the grouped schemes, the exact estimator of the distances, the noise-variance bound, M1s, M1sn, the jackknife+ intervals, the guard bands with their priors and the new family scored in the produced family's floor. A fifth round, of the version prepared for the journal, led to M7n, M7, Mcd, the relation-matched intervals, the decision-time strata, the per-family and out-of-sample guard bands, the costs, the between-run sensitivity, the cross-fitting, the few-shot transfer and the design-level verdicts, and a sixth, of its revision, to the guard bands available when deciding, the band widened by stated costs, the prior sensitivity of M7n and the trial under between-run variation.
