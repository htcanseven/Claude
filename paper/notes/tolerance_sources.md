# General tolerances used in the requirement scenarios

Checked against the text of the standards (identical national adoptions of ISO 2768-1:1989 and ISO 2768-2:1989)
and at least five independent published tables each; cite the standards themselves.

## ISO 2768-1:1989, angular dimensions (shorter leg of the angle, mm)

| Class | up to 10 | >10–50 | >50–120 | >120–400 | >400 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| f, m | ±1° | ±0°30′ | ±0°20′ | ±0°10′ | ±0°5′ |
| c | ±1°30′ | ±1° | ±0°30′ | ±0°15′ | ±0°10′ |
| v | ±3° | ±2° | ±1° | ±0°30′ | ±0°20′ |

The standard covers parts formed from sheet metal. Used for the wall angle (wall about 28 mm high) and the cut
arms (20–40 mm): row >10–50 mm, i.e. f/m 0.5°, c 1°, v 2°.

## ISO 2768-2:1989, straightness and flatness (nominal length, mm)

| Class | up to 10 | >10–30 | >30–100 | >100–300 | >300–1000 | >1000–3000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H | 0.02 | 0.05 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.3 | 0.4 |
| K | 0.05 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.4 | 0.6 | 0.8 |
| L | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.4 | 0.8 | 1.2 | 1.6 |

For flatness the length is the longer lateral length or the diameter of a circular surface (clause 5.1.1), so the
80 mm evaluation circle of the bottom dome falls in >30–100 mm: H 0.1, K 0.2, L 0.4 mm. Caveats: the standard
mainly applies to features produced by material removal, and it was withdrawn in 2021 (replaced by ISO 22081:2021,
which has no class table); it is used as a representative general flatness requirement.

## DIN 6930-2:2011, stamped steel parts

Classes f, m, g, sg. No flatness tolerance (left to agreement). Its angle table is for flat, cut-only stampings;
angles of formed parts produced by bending refer to DIN 6935, ±2° for legs up to 30 mm, equal to ISO 2768-1 class v
for the wall.
