\noindent A signal-to-drift ratio for quantifying winding-fault detectability and selecting condition monitoring across three wind-turbine generator types

\smallskip
\noindent H\"useyin Tayyer Canseven

\begin{itemize}
  \item A signal-to-drift ratio quantifies fault detectability without a model or detector
  \item Phasor residuals remove the faulted-phase dependence of negative-sequence ratios
  \item Signatures relocate across voltage, controller and field current by machine and drive
  \item A decision table selects the cheapest adequate sensor suite with bootstrap confidence
  \item Detectors transfer across generators once healthy-referenced (AUC 0.85 to 0.98)
