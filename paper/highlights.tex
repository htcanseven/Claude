\medskip
\noindent A signal-to-drift ratio for quantifying winding-fault detectability and selecting condition monitoring across three wind-turbine generator types

\smallskip
\noindent H\"useyin Tayyer Canseven

\begin{itemize}
  \item A scale-free signal-to-drift ratio measures fault detectability without a detector
  \item Identical winding faults are three different physical events in PMSG, SCIG and WFSG
  \item The fault signature relocates with the machine--drive combination, not the controller
  \item Voltage transducers lower the PMSG's detectable extent and add nothing for the SCIG
  \item Detectors transfer across generator types only with trial-referenced features
\end{itemize}
