\noindent A signal-to-drift ratio for winding-fault detectability and sensor selection in three wind-turbine generator--drive systems

\smallskip
\noindent H\"useyin Tayyer Canseven

\begin{itemize}
  \item A drift-calibrated statistic quantifies seeded-fault detectability without a model
  \item Phasor residuals remove the faulted-phase dependence of negative-sequence ratios
  \item Signatures relocate across voltage, controller and field current by machine and drive
  \item A decision table selects the cheapest sensor suite meeting power 0.9 at the extent
  \item Detectors transfer across generators once healthy-referenced (AUC 0.85 to 0.98)
\end{itemize}
