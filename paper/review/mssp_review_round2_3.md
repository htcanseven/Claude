# Reviewer 3, round 2 (wind-turbine condition monitoring and PHM practice)

## What changed, as I read it

The revision does most of what the three round-1 reports asked for and the shortening has not hollowed it out. The negative-sequence features are now phasor residuals, the MDE is a bracket on physically distinct fault levels defined from the measured fault current, and the decision table (Table 5) prints bootstrap probabilities of a joint event with a stated power (detectable share 0.9), re-selects the fixed feature in every resample, is monotone over nested suites and separates the five-cycle PMSG/SCIG analysis from the three-cycle WFSG one. False-alarm counts of every fixed feature appear in Table 4, the screen's size and power are stated (lines 146–148), α is translated into false alarms per unit time and the MDEs are called bench limits (lines 215–218). The decomposition is given in relative and absolute form (lines 355–374), the transfer experiment reports TPR at 1 % FPR with cluster-bootstrap intervals and a per-operating-point baseline (Table 6), "transfers only" is withdrawn (lines 581–582), and the limitations (6.3) now carry the list I asked for. The shortening removed the positioning, per-phase, decomposition and appendix tables and folded their numbers into the text; the title is unchanged, the abstract says "laboratory-bench data" (line 25).

## Remaining comments

Major

1. The stated power is pooled over the test matrix (Section 3.3 lines 164–165; step 5 lines 193–195; Table 5 note line 886). DS(e ≥ e_req) is the share of all tested trials at or above e_req, so a suite can approach "meets" at the smallest level because the larger faults, which outnumber it, are all detected. From the per-trial table, the share at the level itself is 64 % for the PMSG voltage residual at 2.7–3.8 % inter-turn (pooled 88 %), 78 % for the PMSG duty-cycle residual at 2.3–3.2 % inter-winding (pooled 96 %) and 78 % for the SCIG duty-cycle residual at the smallest inter-turn level (pooled 93 %); under a per-level power all three "uncertain" cells (0.27, 0.93, 0.82) would fail. An operator writes "power 0.9 at e_req", not over a bench's mix of extents. Required: say in 3.3 and in the Table 5 note that the power is pooled and depends on the matrix composition, add the share at e_req itself for at least the uncertain cells, and rewrite lines 592–594, where "raises the detectable share at the smallest level from 70–75 to 88 %" quotes the pooled share (at the level it is 11–25 % → 64 %). Blocks acceptance as written, because 6.2 and the conclusions are what would be copied into a specification; a minor-revision fix.

2. Lowest monitored speed (lines 513–522). The paper rightly names it a requirement quantity, but the only evidence is a sentence that the released tables do not reproduce (check 9), and the retrofit suite's feature on the PMSG, the current residual, does not rise with speed at all. Required: name the features and window behind lines 513–516, correct the torque statement, and give the detectable share of each adequate suite's fixed feature at 1200 rpm (the bench's lowest speed) for the cells that decide Table 5, or a per-speed line in Table 4; release the per-speed table. Not blocking once corrected.

3. The OEM/retrofit split is still a limitation rather than a result (lines 628–631). Section 4.3 (lines 283–289) still presents one ordinal ranking and calls the 3 CT suite "data access rather than hardware" (line 286), which is the OEM reading only; the Table 4 note "suites are cumulative" (line 840) is false for drive → 3CT, since no drive-internal feature is computable from three currents, and the inheritance rule (lines 894–896) should say which subsets it uses. ## Length and presentation

The 31-page version is readable and, for the main argument, complete without a supplement: Tables 3–6 carry the quantities a practitioner needs and the numbers folded into Sections 5.2, 5.3 and 5.7 reproduce from the repository. Two things the shortening took out should come back in compact form: the per-speed behaviour of the fixed features (major comment 2; nothing in the paper now shows it) and the six-direction three-machine transfer matrix (lines 506–511 give only ranges). The sensitivity and between-trial-null results survive as text ranges, which is acceptable because the CSVs are public, but the Data availability statement should name the table files. Fig. 1 is now clean; Fig. 4 is compact and legible, with the SDR = 1 line marked by the axis label; Fig. 5 makes the faulted-phase point at a glance; Fig. 2 remains decorative. Table 4's "cumulative" note is wrong (major 3), Table 3's column head "S/P ratio" should read "SCIG/PMSG", and the Table 5 note is long but correct and now says what "meets" means.

## Recommendation

Minor revision. Confidence: high. The inferential defects of round 1 are repaired, the claims are bounded to the benches in the abstract, limitations and conclusions, and every number I checked reproduces from the released tables except the five items listed, none of which changes a verdict. What remains is semantic and presentational but matters to the paper's intended reader: the pooled power behind "meets", the unsupported per-speed sentence, the missing retrofit reading of the decision table, and the WFSG overstatement in the conclusions.
