# Reviewer 3 report (wind-turbine condition monitoring and PHM practice)

## Summary of the submission

The paper proposes the signal-to-drift ratio (SDR): the relative change of a feature between a pre-fault reference interval and the faulted interval, divided by the 95th percentile of the healthy change between the two halves of the reference interval, pooled over a machine's trials. From per-trial SDRs it derives a minimum detectable extent (MDE), a detectable share (DS) and a signature location, builds a bootstrap decision table naming the cheapest adequate sensor suite, and tests whether a learned detector transfers between machines. It is applied to the open São Paulo benchmarks: a 2.5 kVA PMSG and a 2.5 kW SCIG on one bench and a 2 kVA WFSG on another, with resistor-limited winding shorts between taps. The main results are that the SCIG's higher SDRs come from a quieter healthy null rather than a larger signature, that the PMSG's inter-turn signature sits in the terminal-voltage negative sequence, and that a detector transfers only when referenced to each trial's own preceding healthy window.

## Assessment against MSSP criteria

**Originality and advance over established methods.** By the author's own account (lines 206–212) the SDR is Cohen's d divided by 1.96 with an empirical null, and the DS is one point of a per-feature ROC. The packaging (pooled null, binomial screen, limit-of-detection MDE, decision table) is sensible engineering, not a signal-processing advance, and the paper never shows what the SDR yields that a per-feature ROC or Fisher separability on the same intervals would not. The genuine contribution is experimental: the controlled PMSG–SCIG comparison, the decomposition of Table 4 and the negative-sequence-impedance explanation (lines 530–538).

**Technical soundness.** The arithmetic reproduces from the released CSVs (Tables 4–8, A.1–A.4). The weaknesses are inferential: the MDE is a 50 %-power quantity, the screen with nine healthy trials has almost no power, decision verdicts are non-monotone over nested suites, and the PMSG inter-winding suite conclusion flips between three- and five-cycle windows.

**Significance and generality.** For a wind-turbine CM practitioner the numbers are bench quantities: resistor-limited faults, 40–60 Hz machines, a 0.68 s healthy reference at a stationary operating point, a 5 % per-trial false-alarm rate, one unit per topology and no DFIG. Section 6.4 is honest about most of this; the title, abstract and highlights are not.

**Clarity and presentation.** The prose is precise but the paper is far too long for its content (about 11 700 words, 5 figures, 12 tables).

**Reproducibility.** Good in principle (public data, released pipeline, seeds listed), but the licence is unspecified and no tagged release or archived DOI exists at submission.

## Major comments

1. **Novelty is overstated (Sections 1, 2.1, 3.2; Table 1).** Table 1 omits the nearest relatives: health-indicator separability and Fisher criteria, Mahalanobis-type detectability indices, and the per-feature ROC of which the DS is admittedly a point. Rank the features on the same trials by per-feature AUC and by Fisher score and state whether any suite verdict changes; if none does, present the SDR as a reporting convention and make the experimental comparison the contribution.

2. **The decision table (Table 7, Section 5.5, step 5) is not usable.** (a) "Meets" rests on the MDE, which by definition (lines 260–263) is the extent at which the median operating point reaches SDR = 1, i.e. 50 % detection power; a specification needs a stated power. (b) The "(suite)?" convention hides the probability: at α = 0.95, e_req = 5 %, inter-turn PMSG prints "(drive)?" although the released table gives p = 0.35 for drive-internal and 0.94 for 3CT+3VT. (c) Verdicts are non-monotone over nested suites: at α = 0.95, 12 %, inter-winding PMSG, 3CT "meets" (0.965) while its superset 3CT+3VT is "uncertain" (0.872). The fixed feature, chosen by median SDR, does not optimise the table's quantity. (d) The table uses three cycles because of the WFSG, yet at five cycles the PMSG inter-winding MDE with VTs is 2.3 % instead of 9.7 % (Table A.2). (e) WFSG verdicts carry no false-alarm screen. Fix: rows = suites, cells = p_meets and DS at e_req, per-machine window length, monotonicity over nested suites, "meets" defined on a required DS, unscreened cells flagged.

3. **The false-alarm side of the specification is effectively unverified (Section 3.2 safeguard (i), Sections 4.4, 5.7, A.2).** With n_h = 9 the binomial rule flags only ≥ 3/9 false alarms; a feature with a true 20 % rate passes with probability 0.74. The PMSG's headline feature V2/V1 fires on 2 of 9 contactor-only trials at three cycles (lines 659–660), 22 % against a nominal 5 %, yet passes. Also, α = 0.95 per 0.36 s trial is roughly one false alarm every 7 s of operation; a field monitor needs 1−α of order 10⁻⁷, which 225 reference intervals cannot estimate, and the sensitivity study stops at 0.99. Report the empirical false-alarm rate of every fixed feature in Tables 6, 7 and A.1, translate α into false alarms per unit time, and call every MDE a bench quantity.

4. **Relevance to multi-megawatt generators (title, abstract, Sections 4.2, 6.2).** The faults are resistor-limited (2.6 Ω across up to 40 % of a branch), so the signature scales with e·ω/R_f; a field inter-turn fault is leakage-reactance-limited with circulating currents of several times rated, so the signature-versus-extent law and hence every MDE differ in kind. The smallest tested extent (2.7–2.8 %) is "not reliably detectable" (lines 556–557) and the paper never says how many turns it is; an incipient fault of one to three turns in a large winding is below 1 % of a branch. The IEA 15 MW scaling (lines 401–402) preserves only the torque–speed shape: electrical frequency, per-unit reactances and the current-loop bandwidth to 2fe ratio are unmatched, and the paper itself expects the signature to move at 10 Hz (lines 745–748). Give turns per branch, justify or drop the IEA framing, retitle to "three generator topologies on laboratory benches", and make Section 6.2 conditional.

5. **Cost ordering and availability of drive-internal quantities (Section 4.3, Table 3, step 1).** The ranking drive < 3CT < 3CT+3VT < 3CT+3VT+drive is not additive (drive costs nothing at rank 0 but one rank when added to 3CT+3VT). Controller states, PI outputs and duty cycles at 2fe bandwidth are not exposed by commercial converters to third-party CM systems: for a retrofit the "drive" column of Table 7 does not exist and three clip-on CTs are the cheapest suite, whereas for an OEM-integrated monitor the VT increment is negligible. Present both scenarios, make cost a user input, and show all adequate suites rather than one "cheapest".

6. **The transfer result is weaker and narrower than claimed (Section 5.6, Tables 8 and A.4, Highlight 5, abstract).** "Transfers" is defined by AUC; at 1 % FPR the true-positive rate into the PMSG is 0.35–0.52 (Table A.4). The "commissioning baseline" is nine trials, so the comparison is tilted. Self-referencing to the first 0.3 s of the same trial is an abrupt-onset detector at a stationary operating point: it cannot see a fault present at commissioning or one that grows slowly, and in a turbine the preceding window carries operating-point wander absent from these data. Report TPR at fixed FPR with intervals (one-cycle-hop windows are strongly correlated), build the baseline from all reference intervals of the target, relabel the finding as abrupt-onset transfer, and delete "only" (GBDT reaches 0.82/0.75 under the baseline rule).

7. **"Signature versus silence" (Section 5.2, Table 4, Section 6.1, Highlights 2–3).** Relative normalisation inflates the null of features whose healthy level is near the floor: PMSG Id 2fe has q95 = 0.56, a 56 % change between adjacent healthy halves, which says the PMSG's healthy 2fe ripple is tiny, not that the SCIG is "quiet". Give healthy absolute levels for the key features and repeat the Table 4 decomposition with the absolute variant before concluding "entirely a quieter null". "Not the controller" is untestable: the drives differ in angle source and necessarily in current-loop tuning. The "three different physical events" finding is a property of resistor-limited emulation, as lines 581–583 admit; demote it to a caveat.

8. **Length and scope.** Cut Section 2.3, the set notation of 3.1, and Sections 3.5 and 6.3 (feasibility-only, outside MSSP's scope); keep either Fig. 1 or the list in 3.4; drop Fig. 2; merge Table 6 into A.1 at one window length; fold Table A.2's five-cycle column into Table 5; collapse Table 8's all/o.o.r. pairs (they agree within 0.02) and shorten A.4.

9. **Limitations to add (Section 6.4).** Fixed thermal state; absent nuisance transients; per-speed false-alarm rates (up to 16 %, SCIG I2/I1 at 1800 rpm); the 50 %-power MDE; the 50 WFSG trials at 1671 rpm with at most two windows; no uncertainty on AUCs; nine healthy trials; and the realistic between-trial null of Table A.3 not being the headline.

## Note to the editor

The subject is within MSSP's health-monitoring scope and the experimental core (a controlled two-machine comparison with a mechanism for the difference) deserves publication somewhere. The desk-rejection risk is nonetheless real: the "method" is a standardised effect size, its method-making sections (3.5, 6.3, the generic framing) are feasibility-only, the decision table has logical defects, and the title and highlights promise wind-turbine conclusions that 2 kVA resistor-limited benches cannot deliver. To be publishable in MSSP the paper must become a roughly 25-page experimental study with the SDR as a reporting convention, a decision table with stated power and false-alarm rates, a fair and honestly labelled transfer experiment, and bench-bounded claims.

## Recommendation

Major revision. The analysis is reproducible and the two-machine comparison is a genuine, if modest, contribution, but the inferential layer (50 %-power MDE, powerless screen, non-monotone verdicts, AUC-only transfer) and the over-reach toward multi-megawatt turbines must be corrected, and the length roughly halved, before it can be judged on its merits; absent that reframing, rejection is appropriate. Confidence: high on the statistical and practical points, medium on the scope judgement.
